Our Story — High-End Interior Editorial

A home that tells a family's story.

We do not begin with a catalogue. We begin with how you live: morning light in the bedroom, homework at the dining table, grandparents in the firmest chair, guests who stay for one more cup of coffee. The furniture follows the life — never the other way round.

Chapter I

The Wood Before the Furniture

Every piece starts long before it is a sofa, bed or table. We choose seasoned American walnut for its deep, calm grain, solid teak for strength in coastal humidity, and warm acacia and mango wood where a lighter, lived-in character suits the room. Cane is hand-woven so air moves through it, brass is left unlacquered so it warms and patinas with touch, and every edge is sanded, oiled and hand-finished — not rushed through a spray line. If the wood is not right, we do not build with it.

Chapter II

Made for the Room It Will Live In

A beautiful piece in the wrong size is not beautiful for long. We measure the wall, the doorway, the window light and the way people actually move through the room. A dining table is sized for the family plus two guests, not for a photograph. A wardrobe is built around the awkward corner, not in spite of it. A lounge chair is placed where the evening light falls. That is why a CASA LIVING room feels settled on the first day — it was designed for that room, in that house, for those people.

Chapter III

Furniture That Outlives Trends

Trends change every season; a family table should not. We favour quiet forms, honest materials and joinery that can be repaired, re-oiled and re-covered, rather than fashion that dates in three years. Walnut deepens, cane softens, brass gains character — the piece grows more itself with use. Buy once, live with it for decades, and one day hand it on. That is the real luxury: furniture you do not need to replace.

The Maker's Note

Made slowly, by hands that know the wood.

Our workshop joins, sands and finishes by hand, checking each frame, hinge and weave before it leaves. A walnut tabletop is matched grain by grain. A cane panel is tensioned so it stays true in humidity. A brass foot is fitted, not glued on as an afterthought. Small imperfections in natural wood are not hidden — they are the proof that the material is real, and that someone took the time to work with it, not around it.

Made-to-Order

Your room is not standard. Your furniture shouldn't be either.

Bring your room measurements, a photo, or just an idea. Our demo custom service designs, quotes and builds it for your exact space.

  1. Measure & discuss (Day 1): Visit or video call — room size, storage needs, wood and fabric choices.
  2. Design & quote (3–5 days): Sketch, materials, finish and a fixed demo price before making starts.
  3. Build (3–5 weeks): Workshop build with progress photos for larger pieces.
  4. Deliver & install: White-glove delivery, placement, packaging removed.
