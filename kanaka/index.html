The Kanaka Story

Not bought in a hurry. Worn for a lifetime.

A piece of jewellery is rarely only metal and stone. It is a wedding morning, a mother's blessing, a first salary, a sixtieth birthday. This is how we make something worthy of that moment.

I

Heritage — Car Street, 1987

Kanaka began as a small workshop near Car Street in Mangaluru, where temple jewellery was made for weddings across Dakshina Kannada and Udupi. The first pieces were not made for a showcase. They were made for a bride who would wear them from morning rituals to a late-night reception — so they had to sit comfortably, close securely, and still catch light in a crowded hall.

That discipline remains: every design is first judged on the wrist, the neck and the ear, not only on paper.

“Gold should feel like it belongs to you the moment it touches your skin — not like something you are afraid to move in.”

II

The Goldsmith's Hand

In the atelier, a temple motif is not stamped and forgotten. The Lakshmi face is shaped, the peacock's feathers are chased line by line, and the edges are polished until they feel smooth against the skin, never sharp. A necklace is weighed in the hand before it is weighed on the scale — a good piece has presence without heaviness, and a clasp that closes with a quiet, certain click.

Only then is it checked twice, BIS hallmarked, and entered on the bill with its weight, purity, stone weight and making charges shown separately. If a stone is set, you are shown the setting before the polish, not after.

III

The Moment It Is Worn

The true test is not in the store. It is at home, in daylight, when a daughter fastens her mother's temple necklace, when a bride looks in the mirror before leaving for the mantap, when a simple gold pendant is worn with an office shirt and still looks right at dinner. A Kanaka piece is finished for that moment: secure enough to forget you are wearing it, luminous enough that others remember it.

FAQ

Questions customers ask us most.

Is your gold BIS hallmarked?

Yes. Every gold piece is BIS hallmarked, and your bill shows gold weight, purity, stone weight, making charges and GST separately.

Can I exchange old gold?

Yes, at 100% of the day's gold value for KANAKA pieces, after a purity check. Old gold from other jewellers is also accepted, with the purity test shown to you.

How long does a custom design take?

Most custom pieces take 10–21 days, depending on design and stone availability. Bridal sets should be ordered at least 4 weeks before the wedding.

Do you deliver outside Mangaluru?

For this demo, pickup is in Mangaluru. In a real store, insured delivery and home trials can be arranged — confirm in store before ordering.

What if a ring size is wrong?

Free resizing within 7 days for most rings, subject to design. Diamond and polki pieces are resized only after a design check, to protect the setting.
